Predictive Maintenancefor Induction Motor Failure


Outline :
An Intelligent On-Premise Predictive Maintenance Platform that continuously learns from operational data, predicts induction motor failures before they occur, and provides actionable maintenance recommendations through flexible industrial connectivity and dynamic machine learning capabilities
Business Problem:
• Current machine monitoring systems detects issues only after signs of degradation have already appeared
• Relying solely on individual thresholds is no longer sufficient in complex system, need multiple operating parameter
Goal:
• Develop an AI-powered Predictive Maintenance solution capable of detecting potential induction failures before they occur.
• The system should continuously analyze historical and real-time data, recognize degradation patterns, estimate failure probability, and provide actionable insights to support proactive maintenance planning.

Key Features to Build:
• Multi-parameter monitoring and analysis (temperature, vibration, electrical current, pressure, and other sensor data).
• AI-based pattern recognition for early detection
• Intelligent false alarm reduction of abnormal machine behavior.by understanding relationships between multiple parameters.
• Remaining Useful Life (RUL) estimation to predict the expected time before failure.
• Real-time health scoring and condition monitoring dashboard.
• Automated alerts and maintenance recommendations.

Expected Impact:
• Earlier detection of potential equipment failures before production is affected.
• Reduced unplanned downtime and maintenance costs.
• Lower false alarm rates compared to traditional threshold-based systems.
• Improved maintenance scheduling through Remaining Useful Life predictions.
• Increased equipment reliability and operational efficiency.
• Better utilization of maintenance resources through data-driven decision making.
